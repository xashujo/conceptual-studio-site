**Measured system:**
- **Ground:** #EDDEC9 (237 222 201) on the home page. Project pages take the client's colour: Oxford #D3AA62, Fou a light grey.
- **Type:**
  - Agatho Regular: 509px ("About"), 407px ("Contact"), 95px ("Say Hello,").
  - Inter: 40/48 bold with −2.1px tracking (card titles), 40/44 semibold (home statement), 20/30 medium with −0.5px tracking (disciplines), 20px semibold (pill labels).
  - Inter Tight: 24px (hero credits, body).
  - Inter: 16px at 50% black (footer).
- **Secondary text:** rgb(125 125 125), neutral.
- **Cards:** 1156px wide, radius 33px, image inset 28px with a 25px corner, background rgb(79 79 79 / 0.02), blur 10px, shadow 0 4px 8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.15). Hairline 1100×1px black. Image 1100px wide.
- **Pills:** radius 30px, background paper at 5%, 1px border at black 5%, blur 2px, shadow 0 4px 8px at 15%.
- **Dock:** fixed, 322×60, radius 15px, blur 7px, background rgb(34 36 38 / 0.05), shadow 0 4px 8px at 20%. 30px from the bottom.
- **Hero media:** two muted WebM videos played once. The gold flow is 4.23s at 1200px wide; the "Studio" signature is 5s at 200px wide. DECISIONS replaces the flow with a still.

**Micro-interactions:** see the table in TASTE.md. Method: a per-frame recorder read the computed transform, opacity, colour, size and shadow of every element under the hovered or clicked control. It re-queried the page each frame because Framer swaps elements between variants. Sequences were also screenshotted at 0, 80, 160, 300 and 600ms.

## 3. Glass in the git history

Commits before the Figma-style glass (9b68f8b, "five layers like the Figma plugin"):

| Commit | Where the glass is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 69b19e3 | Desktop bar and phone dock (CSS plus Chromium SVG refraction); dark WebGL plates over full-colour Lemoyne and Boucher (quote, "Tell me about your project", contact). | Best dark glass. The paintings are full colour, which DECISIONS now forbids. |
| b641b7c | Everywhere: bar, chips, every project card, the hero "viewer", price cards, the form, FAQs, quotes, all over a gold engraving. Adds a gradient rim. | Too much. It breaks "glass only where something sits behind it", and the "N° 01" and "SELECTED WORK 02 / 06" labels come from here. |
| 2a7589f | Chrome only: the bar (plain at the top, glass on scroll) and the phone menu sheet, which is WebGL light glass over the gold engraving. | **Best.** The menu sheet is the DECISIONS pairing exactly. The desktop bar becomes glass over plain paper, which is not allowed. |

`src/scripts/liquid-glass.js` and `src/scripts/refraction.js` are byte-identical in all four commits. The difference is only where the glass is used and its CSS.

**Library:** dashersw/liquid-glass-js (MIT, last commit 12 Jun 2025). It uses WebGL with an html2canvas page snapshot. Defaults: edgeIntensity 0.01, rimIntensity 0.05, baseIntensity 0.01, edgeDistance 0.15, rimDistance 0.8, baseDistance 0.1, cornerBoost 0.02, rippleEffect 0.1, blurRadius 5, tintOpacity 0.2. The old build replaced the snapshot with the known background image and turned warp off (baseIntensity 0).

**iOS 27 Liquid Glass (Apple, WWDC June 2026, as reported):**
- A transparency slider from clear to tinted.
- Better diffusion of complex content behind glass.
- New darkened edges and brighter specular highlights.
- Respects Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.
- A uniform toolbar when content scrolls under floating bars.

Sources: [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/06/10/how-liquid-glass-is-changing-in-ios-27), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/ios-27-has-a-bunch-of-changes-including-a-refined-liquid-glass-design-but-how-much-has-changed-from-ios-26).
