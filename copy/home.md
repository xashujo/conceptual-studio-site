# Home page copy (draft 1)

Rules applied: DECISIONS.md, brief sections 1, 3 and 6.1. Plain British English, "I", no em dashes, none of the banned words.

"Colleges" appears once on the page, in the "Who I work with" line. The headlines say "education" and "public institutions" so they don't use up that one mention.

## Headline options

Pick one. Each has the sentence that sits under it.

### Option A

Brand identity and data visualisation for education and public institutions

I'm Josh McDonald, and Conceptual Studio is my design practice in London. Most of my work is in education, and I also take on commercial projects and work for artists.

Could a marketing head repeat it to their Principal? Yes, almost word for word: "he does brand identity and data visualisation for education and the public sector." It's the most literal option and the longest, so it needs a smaller display size to stay within two lines.

### Option B

Identities and infographics for public institutions

A logo I designed ran on buses and the tube across central and east London for months, and my infographics were used in the executive communications for a college merger. I run Conceptual Studio from London and work with companies and artists too.

Could they repeat it? Yes, and the sentence gives them something to say in the meeting ("his logo was on the buses"). "Infographics" is a plainer word than "data visualisation" for a Principal. Note: this sentence says "college", so if you pick B, the "Who I work with" line below drops the word.

### Option C

Brand identity, reports and data design, mostly for education

Conceptual Studio is me, Josh McDonald, working from London. Whether you're a public institution, a company or an artist, you work with me directly at every stage.

Could they repeat it? Yes. "Mostly for education" tells the Principal I've done this before and still leaves room for everyone else, and "you work with me directly" answers the capacity question that procurement asks of a one-person studio. The risk is that "mostly" can read as hesitant.

### Option D

Logos, annual reports and data maps for institutions

I design identities and the documents and charts that go with them, from strategy papers to sustainability reports. Most of my clients are in education, and the rest are companies and artists who come to me for branding, 3D and motion.

Could they repeat it? Yes, and it's the most concrete: a Principal knows exactly what a logo, an annual report and a map are. It undersells the wider data work a little, since "data maps" sounds narrower than it is.
