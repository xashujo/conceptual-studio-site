## How I work

How a project runs, from the first call to the files you keep.

1. Call and brief
We talk through what you need, your budget, the formats, and what you want the work to achieve, so we both know what to expect before I start.

2. Design and feedback
I share work in progress and we go back and forth on it, so nothing reaches the final round as a surprise.

3. Revisions and finishing
I make your changes and finish every detail before anything is signed off.

4. Handover
You get the final files in whatever format and by whatever method suits your team.
